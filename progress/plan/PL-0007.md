# PL-0007 — Map the preferred design alternative into the BIM graph

**Status:** Active / Executed; review pending  
**Priority:** High  
**Origin:** Follow-up from `AC-0003`.

## Objective

Convert the preferred synthetic design alternative into an explicit, traceable BIM-style element graph linked to the base fixture, while preserving the distinction between candidate design output and professional or regulatory approval.

## Scope

- Use `alternative-a` from `docs/architecture/architecture-evaluation-design-alternatives-v1.json`.
- Create `docs/architecture/architecture-evaluation-selected-design-v1.json`.
- Map levels, spaces/units, elements, materials, views, relationships, revision, provenance, and audit data.
- Mark inferred or synthetic values explicitly.

## Exclusions

- No production application code, database migrations, or runtime generation engine.
- No claim that the Architechtures application produced the mapped graph.
- No IFC export, solid geometry, daylight, parking, accessibility, or regulatory review.
- No modification of the source v1 datasets.

## Acceptance criteria

- Preferred alternative links to explicit BIM-style entities and relationships.
- Mapped entities identify their source alternative or an explicit inference.
- Levels, spaces/units, elements, materials, views, revision, and audit records exist.
- Traceability and limitations are explicit.
