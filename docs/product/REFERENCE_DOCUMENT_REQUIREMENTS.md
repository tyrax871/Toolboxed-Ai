# Reference Document Requirements

This document records requirements inferred from the uploaded Lot 22 Bitola Grove, Lalor references. It is a product reference, not a substitute for professional design, engineering, certification, or regulatory review.

## Accessible reference groups

| Reference group | Product requirements |
|---|---|
| Architectural index | Support a drawing index, general notes, site roof plan, slab layout, ground floor plan, elevations, sections, window schedule, door schedule, electrical layout, tile layout, heating layout, and joinery drawings |
| Building permit | Store project parties, property details, permit identifiers, planning references, building classification, floor area, cost, practitioners, conditions, inspection stages, commencement, completion, and occupancy requirements |
| Geotechnical report | Store site classification, investigation date, bore locations, soil layers, fill depth, drainage conditions, limitations, and recommendations as referenced information linked to site and foundation decisions |
| Truss layout and shop drawings | Store truss identifiers, roof pitch, overhang, spacing, roofing, ceiling, fixings, wind information, tie-downs, dimensions, sheets, and revisions |
| Specifications | Link room and element requirements for kitchen, bathrooms, wet areas, appliances, windows, heating, insulation, plumbing, electrical, ceilings, doors, roofing, framing, services, garage, drainage, footings, allotment, and landscaping |
| Energy-rating references | Store rating number and date, star rating, climate zone, construction assemblies, insulation values, glazing performance, window schedules, orientation, shading, ceiling penetrations, floor finishes, and thermal performance loads |
| Colour, tiling, fixtures, and fittings | Provide structured schedules for materials, finishes, colours, fixtures, fittings, selections, variations, quantities, and room associations |

## Information model implications

The application needs relationships between:

- Documents and source references.
- Drawings and model views.
- Schedules and model elements.
- Rooms and specifications.
- Windows and energy properties.
- Roofs and truss systems.
- Wet rooms and plumbing or tiling overlays.
- Ceilings and electrical or heating overlays.
- Site conditions and structural or foundation decisions.
- Permit requirements and inspection records.
- Revisions, approvals, issue packages, and construction tasks.

## Source fidelity and limitations

- Uploaded reference documents should remain linked to their project record.
- Extracted information should identify its source document and page where possible.
- Conflicting documents must be flagged for professional review rather than silently merged.
- Password-protected engineering PDFs require authorized access before their detailed information can be incorporated.
- Toolboxed must not present extracted reference information as engineering approval or regulatory advice.
